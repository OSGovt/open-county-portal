# Code of conduct

This project serves the public, and the way we treat each other should too.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to software,
  government, or this project. Many contributors are county staff, not
  developers.
- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Critique work, not people. Explain the reason behind a request.
- Write in plain language.
- Respect privacy: never post residents' personal information, or anyone's
  private information.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal attacks
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sexual language or imagery
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others
- Publishing someone's private information
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

All project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events) and
public spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report to the maintainers privately through GitHub's private reporting on
this repository, or by email to conduct@osgovt.com (see `MAINTAINERS.md`).
Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers will review promptly and may warn,
temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code. A maintainer
involved in a report steps aside from deciding it.
